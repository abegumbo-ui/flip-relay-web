First time: connect and choose a PIN

Where to find these: console.firebase.google.com → your project → Build → Realtime Database (the address is at the top of the Data tab, like https://something-default-rtdb.firebaseio.com). The secret: gear icon → Project settings → Service accounts → Database secrets → Show.

The secret is never put in this file and never sent anywhere except to Firebase. It is saved in this browser, locked with your PIN.

Enter your PIN

Forgot it? The PIN cannot be recovered, but nothing is lost: it only locks the saved Firebase secret on this device. "Forgot PIN" clears that and lets you enter the Firebase address and secret again with a new PIN. Your messages and rooms are not touched.

Devices

Every phone and tablet that uses one of your pairing codes. A new phone does nothing (no sync, no uploads) until you press Approve. With "Approve new tablets" on, a tablet or web page that joins a code also waits for your Approve.
